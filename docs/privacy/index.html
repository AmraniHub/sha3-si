Privacy notice

Last updated 2026-11-07.

Who we are

sha3.si is operated by Servolia LLC, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA (the controller). Contact for any privacy question: hello@sha3.si.

What we collect, and why

  • Websites you ask us to scan, and the public pages we fetch from them, with their fingerprints and the resulting report. Purpose: to provide the scan and keep verifiable evidence. These are business websites, not personal data, unless a page itself contains some.
  • Your details, if you send the form: email, name, company, role and message. Purpose: to answer your request (our legitimate interest, and your consent to be contacted). We do not add you to a mailing list.
  • Payment details are handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email, billing address and VAT number to invoice you (contract and legal obligation).
  • Your IP address is never stored in clear. A salted hash is kept for about an hour to limit abuse of the free scan (legitimate interest).

No cookies and no tracking on the MiCA service. Your browser may store your email locally, on your device only, to pre-fill forms.

Who processes it for us

  • Vercel Inc. (application hosting; scans run in Frankfurt, Germany)
  • Turso (database)
  • Anthropic PBC (Claude reads the public text of scanned pages; no personal data is sent)
  • Stripe (payments and invoices)
  • Google (email)
  • GitHub (hosting of these static pages)

Some of these providers are in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Reports and evidence: as long as the service runs, because evidence is only useful if it lasts. Form messages: two years after our last exchange. Invoices: as long as tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, object to its use, or ask for a copy, by writing to hello@sha3.si. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
