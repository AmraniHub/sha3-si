1. The service

sha3.si reads public websites from servers in the European Union and reports what they show against website duties in Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCA) and ESMA guidance. The free scan is automated. The audit adds a browser-rendered pass and a person's review, delivered within 48 hours of payment unless we agree otherwise.
